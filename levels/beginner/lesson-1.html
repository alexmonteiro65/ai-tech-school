What is Claude AI and why it matters

Before you write a single prompt, it helps to know exactly what you're talking to — and what it's actually good at.

1

Explanation

Claude is an AI model made by Anthropic that can read, write, reason through problems, and hold a conversation in plain language. Unlike a search engine, it doesn't fetch existing pages — it reads what you give it and generates an original, reasoned response based on that.

What Claude is good at

  • Drafting and editing writing — emails, reports, code, plans
  • Explaining a concept in plain language, at whatever depth you need
  • Summarizing long documents into the parts that matter
  • Reasoning through a problem step by step with you

What Claude is not

  • It doesn't know real-time events unless it's told, or given a tool that can look them up
  • It can make mistakes — confidently stating something incorrect, sometimes called a "hallucination"
  • It has no personal opinions or consciousness — it predicts a helpful response based on patterns learned from text
Why it matters: this changes how work gets done. Instead of doing every step by hand, you describe the outcome you want and collaborate with Claude to get there faster — which is the entire premise of every lesson after this one.
