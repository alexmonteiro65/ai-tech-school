Your first prompt — how to talk to AI correctly

A prompt is just an instruction. Good instructions get good results — this lesson shows you how to write one.

1

Explanation

A prompt is the instruction you give Claude. Vague prompts get vague results. Specific prompts get results you can actually use.

The four-part formula

  • Task — what you actually want done
  • Context — the details Claude needs to do it well
  • Format — how you want the answer shaped (length, structure)
  • Tone (optional) — the voice you want it written in

Compare these two prompts:

Vague: "Write about dogs" Specific: "Write a 100-word paragraph for a children's book about why dogs make loyal pets, in a warm, simple tone."
Tip: treat the first response as a draft, not a final answer. If it's not quite right, refine your prompt with more detail rather than starting over completely.
