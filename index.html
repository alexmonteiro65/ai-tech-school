AI Tech School exists because most AI content is either too shallow to be useful or too dense to get through. Alex built ATS to close that gap — a curriculum modeled directly on how Anthropic teaches Claude itself, organized the way Codecademy organizes skill paths: in order, with clear milestones, and nothing you can't actually do.

Every lesson exists because it unblocks the next one. Beginner builds the mental model. Intermediate gets your hands on real tools. Expert puts it all together into something you could actually run.
