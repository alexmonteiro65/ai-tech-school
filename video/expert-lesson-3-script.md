## English

Hi, I'm Alex, founder of AI Tech School. This is the last lesson of the entire curriculum, and it's where everything comes together.

A real automated "business" pipeline is just three parts: a Trigger, an Agent — with tools via MCP or Composio — and an Output, running without you manually operating each step. Let's walk it end to end. A new lead fills out a form — that's the Trigger. An agent reads the lead, researches the company, and drafts a personalized outreach email, using Claude plus a web tool — that's the Agent. The email sends, the lead is logged in a CRM, and the team gets notified via Gmail, Notion, and Slack — that's the Output.

Before anything like this goes live, do a real deployment check. Start with a human-reviewed version before going fully autonomous. Monitor the first runs closely. And keep an off-switch.

Here's the thing worth sitting with for a second: this lesson is the endpoint of everything you've done across all three paths. Beginner gave you the mental model of Claude. Intermediate gave you Claude Code and a real connector. Expert combines all of it into a multi-tool, guardrailed, autonomous pipeline — exactly like this one.

On the page, you'll assemble your own pipeline and confirm your deployment checklist, then finish with three quick questions. That completes the entire AI Tech School curriculum. Congratulations — and thank you for building this with me.
