# Content calendar — @ai.tech.school, first 30 days

**Manual posting plan — no automation.** No Buffer, Later, or Composio
connector is wired up. That would need a paid scheduling account, API
credentials, and a live-connected Instagram Business account — none of
which this project handles (see CLAUDE.md). Post these yourself, by hand.

## Cadence and timing

3 posts per week, spread across the week rather than clustered — **Tuesday,
Thursday, Sunday**. Times are backed by 2026 posting-time research (Tue/Thu
mornings and evenings, Sunday evenings, are consistently strong across
niches) but this account has zero data of its own on day 1. Check Instagram
Insights after ~2 weeks and shift these times to match your actual
audience — the research is a starting point, not a guarantee.

## Before day 1

Follow the 20 accounts in `accounts-to-follow.md` first — a brand-new
account following relevant, real accounts before its first post tends to
help early discovery. Set up the profile (`profile-setup.md`: picture, bio,
link) before Post 1 goes up, not after.

## After week 4

You'll have a full week of real Instagram Insights data by then. Rebuild
this calendar around your actual audience's active hours instead of the
generic research this first month leaned on — and decide whether new feed
graphics or more Reels make sense next, based on which format performed
better.
